About Investing Copilot

Investing Copilot helps individual investors organize holdings, track performance, maintain watchlists, and capture research in one place. Designed for long-term investors who value clarity, discipline, and informed decision-making.

Research & Planning

Save investment ideas, maintain research notes, track watchlists and evaluate opportunities over time. Set goals and understand timelines to achieve them.

Independent & Transparent

Developed in Philadelphia for individual investors who want to research, plan, and learn with greater clarity. Investing Copilot brings portfolio context, research notes, goals, and watchlists together without the distractions of a trading platform.

Built for Research, Not Trading

Investing Copilot is an information, organization, and planning tool. It does not execute trades, hold customer funds, or provide individualized investment advice. Investment decisions remain with the user and should reflect personal objectives, circumstances, and independent judgment.
